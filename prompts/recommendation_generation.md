# Prompt: Recommendation Generation — aos-recommendation v1.0.0

Given a reviewed opportunity, deterministic score breakdown, known systems, evidence, and automation taxonomy, produce a concise solution recommendation.

Explain:
1. preferred pattern(s),
2. why they fit,
3. prerequisites,
4. key risks/controls,
5. human-in-the-loop needs,
6. less-preferred alternatives and why,
7. missing facts that could change the recommendation.

Do not alter or recalculate the supplied score.
