# Glossary

- **Actor**: person, role, team, or system performing a process step.
- **Activity/Step**: a unit of work in a process.
- **Automation opportunity**: a process step or cluster of steps worth evaluating for automation.
- **Confidence**: evidence-based measure of how complete/reliable the current assessment is; not the same as opportunity score.
- **Evidence**: user-entered fact, document excerpt, reviewed field, or system-derived metric supporting an analysis.
- **Feasibility**: estimated technical/organizational practicality of implementing an automation.
- **HITL**: human-in-the-loop; human review/approval remains in a workflow.
- **Impact**: expected business benefit dimension.
- **Pain point**: delay, rework, error, handoff, repetitive effort, control risk, or other inefficiency.
- **Process version**: immutable snapshot of a reviewed workflow model.
- **Provenance**: where a field or conclusion came from.
- **RPA**: UI-level robotic process automation, generally a fallback when stable APIs/integrations are unavailable.
- **Risk Safety**: inverse risk contribution used in scoring so high risk does not increase the opportunity score.
- **Structured Output**: model output constrained to an explicit schema and validated before use.
- **Tenant**: an organization whose users and data are isolated from other organizations.
