# Mini Project Charter

## Objective
In 18 days, deliver a working demonstrable MVP that converts a business-process description into a reviewed process model and a prioritized, explainable automation opportunity assessment.

## SMART targets
- Complete one end-to-end flow on at least 5 representative demo processes.
- 100% of displayed final scores produced by deterministic code.
- 100% of final opportunities show evidence + score breakdown + confidence.
- Golden eval set passes defined critical schema/evidence gates before demo freeze.
- Deploy a stable demo environment by Day 17.

## Stakeholders
Student team; academic reviewers; ZyGen mentors/stakeholders if involved; demo process owners; future internship engineering/product reviewers.

## Constraints
4 people, 18 days, theme/company requirements may change, limited enterprise system access, limited validated business data.

## Success
A credible decision-support product with strong software-engineering process, not the number of buzzword technologies used.
