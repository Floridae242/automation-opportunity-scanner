# Product Roadmap

## MVP / Hackathon
Single-organization demo, text intake, structured extraction, review, scoring, recommendations, dashboard, report.

## v1 / Internship continuation
Robust document ingestion, comments/review workflow, RBAC, audit explorer, organization settings, reusable automation pattern library, stronger AI evals, asynchronous jobs, exports, production monitoring.

## v1.5
Portfolio analytics, integration catalog, cost templates, industry-specific question sets, approval workflow, configurable scoring weights with versioning.

## v2 / SaaS scale
Enterprise SSO, advanced tenant policies, regional deployment/data residency options, customer-managed retention controls, organization-specific automation catalogs, workflow discovery connectors, portfolio planning and benefit tracking after implementation.

## Explicit scaling rule
Do not split services or introduce new infrastructure because the roadmap says “scale”. Introduce it after telemetry shows a bottleneck or clear security/ownership boundary.
