# Product Roadmap

## Completed MVP / Hackathon
Single-organization demo, text intake, structured extraction, review, scoring, recommendations, dashboard, reports, safe document upload, exports, automated verification, and CI.

## Partially completed v1 / Internship continuation
Comments/review workflow, RBAC, audit explorer, organization administration, exports, and benefit realization are implemented. The remaining v1 work is robust document ingestion, reusable automation pattern library, stronger AI evaluation, asynchronous jobs, and production monitoring.

## Partially completed v1.5
Portfolio analytics, integration catalog, and versioned scoring weights are implemented. The remaining work is cost templates, industry-specific question sets, and approval workflow.

## v2 / SaaS scale
OIDC deployment readiness and benefit tracking are implemented. Remaining work is verified enterprise SSO login, advanced tenant policies, regional deployment/data residency options, customer-managed retention controls, organization-specific automation catalogs, workflow discovery connectors, and portfolio planning.

## Explicit scaling rule
Do not split services or introduce new infrastructure because the roadmap says “scale”. Introduce it after telemetry shows a bottleneck or clear security/ownership boundary.
