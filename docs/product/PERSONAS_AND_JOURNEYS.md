# Personas and User Journeys

## Business Analyst (primary operator)
Needs a guided intake, editable process steps, confidence indicators, and a defensible report. Frustration: unstructured interviews and inconsistent analysis templates.

## Process Owner
Knows operational details. Needs simple review prompts, not technical jargon. Must be able to correct frequency, duration, exceptions, systems, and controls.

## Automation Engineer
Needs integration constraints, data quality, systems involved, volumes, exception rates, and why the product suggested API/workflow/RPA/AI.

## Manager / Executive
Needs prioritized opportunities, expected benefit ranges, risk, confidence, and the assumptions that could invalidate ROI.

## Main journey
1. Analyst creates project and process.
2. Adds text/questionnaire/document content.
3. System extracts a draft.
4. Analyst/process owner corrects the draft.
5. System detects pain points and opportunities.
6. Deterministic scoring produces rank + confidence.
7. Engineer refines feasibility/effort.
8. Manager reviews portfolio/report.
